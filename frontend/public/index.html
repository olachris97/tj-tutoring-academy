Questions parents ask

FAQs

A few quick answers before you book.

Is tutoring fully online?

Yes. Lessons are delivered live online, so students can learn from home wherever they are located.

Can you follow our curriculum?

Yes. We can discuss US, UK, Canadian, Australian, Irish, New Zealand and other English-language programmes.

How do you choose a tutor?

We consider subject expertise, level, curriculum familiarity, availability and the student's learning needs.

Can my child have lessons twice a week?

Yes. Lesson frequency can be arranged around the student's goals and the tutor's availability.

Do you offer exam preparation?

Yes. We can build lessons around revision, past papers, exam technique and targeted topic gaps.

How do we get started?

Send us your details through the free assessment form. We'll review the request and contact you with the next step.
