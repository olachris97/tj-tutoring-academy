Service Agreement

The practical terms for families using TJ Tutoring Academy for ongoing online tutoring.

Last updated · 12 August 2026
This agreement makes the practical side of tutoring clear before lessons begin and can be supplemented by a written quote or arrangement for a particular student.

1. The arrangement

TJ Tutoring Academy will provide online tutoring based on the student's subjects, level, curriculum, schedule and goals agreed with the parent or guardian. The parent or guardian agrees to provide accurate information about the student's needs and relevant school or examination requirements.

2. Sessions

Sessions are normally delivered online at the agreed time. The student should be ready with the required device, connection and learning materials. Tutors may set practice work or recommend resources where appropriate.

3. Curriculum and progress

Lessons can be tailored to recognised school programmes and assessment approaches, including UK, US, Canadian, Australian, Irish, New Zealand and other English-language curricula. Tutors may provide short progress notes about what was covered, areas that need more practice and the next focus.

4. Fees

The applicable lesson fee, currency and payment schedule will be confirmed before lessons begin. Where payment is required in advance, unpaid sessions may be paused until the account is brought up to date.

5. Rescheduling

If a student cannot attend, please contact us as early as possible. We will try to move the session where the tutor's availability allows. Repeated late cancellations or missed sessions may be treated according to the specific booking arrangement.

6. Tutor absence

If a tutor cannot attend, we will make reasonable efforts to reschedule the session or provide another suitable arrangement. We will communicate material changes as soon as reasonably possible.

7. Safeguarding and conduct

Students, parents and tutors are expected to communicate respectfully. Any concern about a student's safety or welfare should be raised promptly with the Academy. We may suspend or end a tutoring arrangement where conduct creates a serious safety or professional concern.

8. No guaranteed result

Tutoring is designed to improve understanding, confidence and exam readiness, but no tutor or Academy can guarantee a specific mark, grade or examination result.

9. Ending the arrangement

Either side may ask to end ongoing tutoring. We will settle any outstanding sessions or credits according to the payment arrangement that applies.

10. Questions

If anything in this agreement is unclear, please ask before the first paid session. Contact support@tjtutoring.academy.
